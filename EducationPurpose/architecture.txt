This is the architecture of Tuned Chaos

The three layers: frontend/ (QML) talks to wrapper/ (Qt C++), and wrapper/ talks to backend/ (plain C++).
Left out: build/ and dependencies/ (generated, not part of the code).
"its implementation" means the .cpp of the .h above it. (TunedChaosMap.pdf shows how the parts connect.)
